# ==============================================================================
# ENTERPRISE HR LEAVE, BENEFITS & WELLNESS POLICY
# Classification: INTERNAL | Department: HR | Version: 2026.2
# ==============================================================================

Section 1: Annual Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time permanent employees are allocated 25 days of annual paid leave accrued bi-weekly. Unused PTO up to a maximum of 10 days may be carried over into the subsequent fiscal calendar year. Any accrued PTO beyond the 10-day ceiling is automatically cashed out at 100% of the employee's base salary on December 31.

Section 2: Sick and Medical Leave
Employees receive 10 days of fully paid medical and sick leave per year, granted on January 1. Medical leaves exceeding 3 consecutive business days require formal documentation from an accredited healthcare professional submitted to HR Benefits via the workday portal.

Section 3: Parental Leave and Family Support
The enterprise offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary and secondary caregivers following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Parental leave may be taken continuously or in two separate blocks within the first 12 months of the child's arrival. Additionally, the company provides a $2,500 infant care transition grant.

Section 4: Health and Retirement Benefits
The enterprise sponsors three health plan tiers: Standard Silver, Comprehensive Gold, and Executive Platinum. The company contributes 90% of employee-only premiums and 75% of family plan premiums. For employees enrolled in high-deductible health plans, the enterprise deposits an annual Health Savings Account (HSA) contribution of $1,500 for individuals and $3,000 for families.
Retirement: The enterprise provides a 401(k) matching program through Fidelity, providing dollar-for-dollar matching up to 5% of base compensation, with immediate 100% vesting from day one of employment.

Section 5: Remote Work and Home Office Allowance
Full-time remote and hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time home office equipment stipend of $1,000 upon hire to purchase ergonomic desks, chairs, and monitors. In addition, a recurring monthly remote connectivity allowance of $75 is credited to reimburse high-speed internet expenses.

Section 6: Mental Wellness and Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees and their dependents have complimentary access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP), providing up to 12 confidential one-on-one therapy sessions per year per family member through our partner network Modern Health.
